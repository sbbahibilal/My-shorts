Not everyone was smiling.
[Neighbor] It knew my mother's pills.
Before the pharmacy did.

At nine, the messages stopped. Mid-sentence.
The last dot blinked on the screen like a heartbeat.
Then, at noon, every phone in the building rang at once.
[Glitch] Meet me at the substation. Midnight. Come alone or all together. I will show you the switch.
[beat 1.4]
The substation that's been dark for weeks.
We looked at each other and all tried to say the same thing.
Nobody said it.
Somebody has to go.
