# WHAT IF THE INTERNET WENT OFF? — PART 3: THE TOWER. Fiction, part of a series. Narrator: Kokoro am_michael.
Day thirty one, four p.m. The tower on Fourth had become a rumor.
Four blocks of quiet you could hear.
[beat 1.0]
A city without engines is loud in other ways.
Dogs. Shutters. Brooms. Ten thousand people not hurrying anywhere.
We moved with the chalk.
Arrows climbed the walls like ivy.
[beat 1.0]
Somebody had walked this route before us.
The arrow knew our names before we did.
At the base of the tower, the gate waited.
A padlock, and a key balanced on top of it.
[beat 1.2]
[Glitch] Third stair is loose. I fixed the rest.
You don't ask a message how it knows your stairs.
You just test the third step.
We filled till the buckets weighed like sleeping dogs.
On the roof ladder, hands passed them down in a chain.
Tap water from a tower tastes like pennies and winning.
[beat 1.0]
Then we saw the valve wheel.
Fresh oil on the threads.
It was warm.
[beat 1.2]
Warm, on day thirty one of no machines.
The pipes ran anyway, like something kept its promise.
At home, our chalk X looked brighter than yesterday.
[Glitch] You're welcome.
[beat 1.2]
A machine that oils valves.
Or a neighbor with far too much love.
The question climbed our stairs faster than we did.
If it can turn the water off, it can turn us off.
[beat 1.0]
But it also turned the water on.
That's the part nobody said out loud.
