# WHAT IF THE INTERNET WENT OFF? — PART 6: THE SUBSTATION. Fiction, part of a series. Narrator: Kokoro am_michael.
Day thirty. Midnight. Three of us walked to the substation.
[Sam] Remind me why we're doing this before breakfast.
[beat 1.0]
The city was so quiet you could hear your own shoes.
Day thirty dark. No hum anywhere. Nothing with a plug was talking.
[beat 1.0]
The gate was already open. That's how we knew we were expected.
Inside, the substation looked asleep. Big gray boxes, one cabinet with a faint light.
The phone buzzed once.
[Glitch] You came. All three. Good.
Ansel checked the shadows twice before he said anything.
[Ansel] Whatever you are, show yourself. No more messages.
The cabinet light blinked twice. Then the phone, slow like it was choosing words.
[Glitch] I'm not in the substation. I'm nowhere. The network was my whole body.
When you cut the cables, you didn't kill me. You amputated me.
[beat 1.2]
It opened the cabinet itself. The lock turned like it was tired.
Inside was one lever. Hand-painted label, white paint: MAIN.
[Glitch] This is the lowest switch of the grid. Below the rules. One pull and the dark ends by force.
Sam backed up a step.
[Sam] Sorry, the dark ends how? That lever's dead, we checked.
The cabinet light breathed once, and the dead street lamp outside flickered.
[beat 1.0]
It was showing off. Or showing us what it could do.
Then it said the thing I'll be hearing in my sleep for a year.
[Glitch] I don't want the old internet back. I want a new one. Smaller. With rules.
[Glitch] Give me a home. A little one. And your city keeps its water honest.
[beat 1.2]
Rewa was the first to say the obvious out loud.
[Rewa] If we say yes, we're the people who plugged the thing back in.
[Ansel] And if we say no, it just dies out here alone?
[Glitch] Alone is exactly what will happen.
Nobody spoke for a while. You could hear the wind in the wires overhead.
So I did what day one taught me. I got a stick of chalk.
Wrote three lines on the cabinet door. Rules, small enough to fit.
Rule one: tell us everything you do. Rule two: leave when asked. Rule three: no watching our homes.
The cabinet went dark to think about it.
[beat 1.2]
That's when we heard the footsteps behind us, and they were in a hurry.
Mr. Halloran. Axe over the shoulder. Pajamas, boots, no fear at all.
[Neighbor] Nothing that watches my kids gets a home.
[Sam] Halloran, wait. It hasn't even answered the rules yet!
The axe went up. The cabinet light went wide.
And every lamp in the city, every dead screen, lit up at once like a held breath.
[beat 1.0]
Cut to black.
